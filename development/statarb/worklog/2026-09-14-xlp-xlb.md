## What was tested

The screen found `XLP~XLB` on 2019-01 to 2026-09, which was the whole of the stored
history. Both funds launched in December 1998, so there were twenty years of daily bars
available that the pair had never been fitted on. Downloading them is the test the plan
said had to happen before Step 3, and it is decisive.

### Cointegration

| Window | Bars | Hedge ratio | Engle-Granger p | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2019-01 to 2026-09, the window it was found in | 1,934 | +0.594 | 0.033 | cointegrated |
| 1998-12 to 2018-12, never fitted on | 5,037 | +0.865 | 0.418 | not cointegrated |
| 1998-12 to 2026-09, everything | 6,972 | +0.928 | 0.101 | not cointegrated |

Split the record into seven independent four-year eras and **none of them reject**:

| Era | Hedge ratio | p | Era | Hedge ratio | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1999–2002 | +0.200 | 0.653 | 2015–2018 | +0.245 | 0.221 |
| 2003–2006 | +0.454 | 0.096 | 2019–2022 | +0.531 | 0.128 |
| 2007–2010 | +0.356 | 0.707 | 2023–2026 | +0.582 | 0.131 |
| 2011–2014 | +0.968 | 0.321 | | | |

Four-year eras have less power than an eight-year window, so that comparison is not fair
on its own. Sliding a window of exactly the winning length (1,934 bars) through the whole
record, a quarter at a time, gives **4 rejections out of 80 windows. Chance alone on a
true null gives 4.0.** The windows overlap heavily, so 80 is not 80 independent tests, but
the finding does not survive in either direction: the rejections sit in two short clusters,
and the independent four-year eras reject nothing at all.

### The hedge ratio is not stable

The earlier note recorded the ratio as stable, +0.541 to +0.594, and used that stability to
separate this pair from `GBPUSD~USDNOK`. That measurement was taken entirely inside the
2019–2026 window. Across the full record the ratio runs **+0.079 to +1.343**, a factor of
seventeen, and the rolling estimator spends 6% of the sample at or below zero — both legs
on the same side. On the twenty unseen years the hedge quality gate fails outright for the
static and rolling estimators.

### Money

Backtests are walk-forward already: the hedge is refit every 5 bars on a trailing window,
so nothing is fitted on data the decision has not seen.

| Window | Trades | Gross | Carry | Net | Sharpe | Max drawdown |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 2019–2026, the fitted window | 18 | +2,373 | −286 | **+2,033** | 0.68 | −486 |
| 1998–2018, never fitted on | 55 | +528 | −754 | **−391** | −0.03 | −1,976 |
| 1998–2026, everything | 75 | +3,020 | −1,058 | **+1,737** | 0.11 | −1,976 |

Twenty-eight years of walk-forward trading returns +1,737 bps, about 62 bps a year, against
a drawdown of 1,976 bps. The drawdown is larger than thirty years of profit. All of the
profit comes from the window the pair was selected in; the twenty years before it lose
money, and lose it the same way everything else in this project has lost it — gross +528
against −754 of financing.

### The parameter surface

The headline is one cell of a grid. Holding the pair fixed and varying only the hedge fit
window and the entry threshold, over the full 28 years:

| | entry 1.5 | entry 2.0 | entry 2.5 | entry 3.0 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **window 150** | −133 | +1,608 | −368 | −230 |
| **window 250** | +3,747 | +1,737 | −2,878 | −1,194 |
| **window 400** | −3,678 | −3,194 | −2,414 | −1,378 |

Three cells of twelve are positive. The mean is **−698 bps** and the range is −3,678 to
+3,747. The sign of the result is decided by the parameters, not by the pair.

### Two things that did check out

**The fill-lag guard passes.** Delaying the fill makes results monotonically worse —
+1,737 at one bar, +145 at two, −660 at three — so there is no look-ahead. But losing 92%
of the net to a single day of delay says the edge lives almost entirely in the bar right
after the signal, which is not something a daily-bar strategy can rely on surviving real
execution.

**Hourly bars fix the financing.** On 1h bars over two years the same idea nets +777 with
carry of only −75, against −1,058 on daily, because the average hold is 2.5 days rather
than 16. That is the first time in this project that financing has not eaten the result.
It is not evidence for this pair — the two years sit inside the one favourable era, and
widening the fit window from 250 to 1,000 bars turns +777 into −81 — but the mechanism is
worth carrying forward.

## What this changes

`XLP~XLB` joins `SPY~DIA`, `GBPUSD~USDNOK`, `AMGN~LLY` and roughly 850 other studies.
Nothing has passed. That is the honest state of the search.

There was never an economic reason for this pair. Consumer staples and materials producers
share no cash flow, no input, no customer and no arbitrage link; the only thing binding
them is that both are equities. Two insurers or two refiners at least have a story. The
screen has no way to ask for one, which is why it found this.

Three things follow, in order:

1. **Require a reason, not just a p-value.** Restrict screening to pairs with a shared
   driver — same industry, same input cost, one instrument holding the other. This narrows
   the search, and a narrower search is a smaller multiple-testing problem.
2. **Test the holding-period idea directly.** Financing has killed every candidate so far,
   and the hourly run is the first sign that a shorter hold escapes it. That is a question
   about bar size and exit rules, not about any one pair.
3. **Apply this treatment to `ALL~TRV` before it is trusted.** It has not been re-tested on
   unseen windows at all, and it is the only remaining candidate.

Step 3 stays paused, and now for a better reason than before: there is no candidate.
